How it works

01Set up

Install and connect

Run the installer, follow the setup wizard, paste your API keys and pick your wheel button. The systems check goes green when everything answers.

02Race

One button on the wheel

Ask him anything — gaps, fuel, who's quicker, when to box. He also calls the important things on his own, and keeps quiet when you need to focus.

03Remember

He learns your series

After each race he writes a report. Next time you race that series, he already knows how it went.

On the pit wall

Live race book

Every car, every lap: times, positions, stops. Nothing is forgotten, nothing is invented.

Fuel & strategy

Knows the series rules and the pit-service rules of each championship — fuel only, simultaneous service, no refuelling.

Radio modes

Direct, Normal or Competition: from chatty to critical-only. Change it by voice mid-race.

Two personalities

Classic: dry, deadpan, all business. Veteran: warm, passionate, full of war stories. Same engineer, same data.

Spotter

Instant car-left / car-right calls, independent from the AI, so they're never late.

English & Spanish

Full app and engineer in both languages. In Spanish he answers to Vicen.

Pit-wall panel

Timing, sectors, stints, strategy and the radio log on a second screen — styled like a real timing wall.

Made for streaming

Radio overlay for OBS and optional Twitch chat: viewers can talk to your engineer.

FAQ

Which sims does it support?

iRacing only, for now.

What do I need?

A Windows PC, iRacing, a microphone, and an internet connection. VINCENT uses cloud AI and voice services, so you bring your own API keys (OpenRouter, Fish Audio, Deepgram) — the setup wizard checks them for you.

How much does a race cost to run?

It depends on the AI model you choose in settings — cheaper models cost cents per race, the best ones a bit more. The app shows token usage live so you always know.

Is it allowed in official races?

VINCENT only reads the telemetry iRacing exposes to every app and talks to you. It never touches the car or the sim.

Windows says “unknown publisher”

The first time, SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info → Run anyway. After that VINCENT updates itself: it downloads new versions when it opens and installs them when it closes.
