A real voice on the radio

Gaps, fuel, who’s quicker, when to box: just ask. VINCENT answers out loud in a natural voice, and calls the big moments on his own — the start, a car slowing ahead, a flag, a rival pitting.

  • Push-to-talk on any wheel button

02

He sees the whole field

VINCENT keeps a lap-by-lap book of every car: lap times, positions, gaps, stints and pit stops. He knows who’s closing in, who’s fading and who hasn’t stopped yet. Nothing forgotten, nothing made up.

  • Rival sectors measured by the app itself
  • Multiclass aware: your race is your class
  • A full timing wall on a second screen

03

Strategy that knows the rules

Fuel per lap, laps left in the tank, when to box and what to take. VINCENT knows each series’ pit rules — fuel only, simultaneous service, no refuelling — and follows your team briefing for endurance and team events.

  • Briefs the incoming driver at every swap
  • Writes a report after each race and remembers it next time

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I use it in official races?

Yes — that’s what it’s built for. VINCENT works like the spotter and overlay apps drivers already use in official races: it only reads the telemetry iRacing exposes to every app, and talks to you. It never touches the car or the sim.

Is it really free?

Yes. VINCENT costs nothing and has no paid version — all I ask is that you follow my Twitch channel, and you keep following while you use it. The only cost is the AI and voice usage, which you pay straight to OpenRouter — I don’t get a cent of it.

How much does a race cost to run?

It depends on the AI model you pick in settings: cheaper models cost cents per race, the best ones a bit more. The app shows your spend live, so there are no surprises.

What do I need?

A Windows PC, iRacing, a microphone, an internet connection and a Twitch, Discord or Google account to sign in. Plus one OpenRouter API key — the setup wizard walks you through it.

Why do I need to sign in?

To download VINCENT and to use it. It tells us who’s using it, stops anyone reselling it, and lets us switch off a broken version fast. Each account works on up to 3 PCs, which you can manage from your account. If your internet drops mid-race, it keeps working.

Where does my API key go?

It’s stored in the Windows Credential Manager on your PC and only ever sent to OpenRouter. It never reaches us. Tip: create a key just for VINCENT and give it a credit limit in OpenRouter, so a lost key can never cost you more than that.

Which sims does it support?

iRacing only, for now.

Windows says “unknown publisher”

The first time, SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info → Run anyway. After that VINCENT updates itself: it downloads new versions when it opens and installs them when it closes.
