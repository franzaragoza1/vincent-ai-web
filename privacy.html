Privacy

VINCENT is a free, independent project. To download and use it you sign in with Discord. This page says exactly what that stores and why.

What we store

  • From Discord (scope “identify” only): your user ID, username, display name and avatar. Not your email, servers or messages.
  • For each PC where you sign in to the app: the PC name, an anonymous fingerprint (a salted hash, not your hardware ID), the VINCENT version and when it last checked in.
  • A simple log of sign-ins, downloads and licence checks, to spot abuse.

What we never get

  • Your API keys (your OpenRouter key and any other you add). They stay in the Windows Credential Manager on your PC and are only sent to the service they belong to.
  • Your telemetry, your radio conversations or your recordings. Those go from your PC to the AI and voice services you configured, never to us.
  • Any payment details. VINCENT is free.

Why

Only to give you access, to limit each account to a few PCs so it can't be shared or resold, to block abusive accounts and to push urgent fixes. We don't sell or share any of it, and this website has no ads or trackers.

Where

The account data lives in Cloudflare (Workers and D1), which hosts the account server.

Your rights

You can see and remove your PCs at any time in your account. To get a copy of your data or have your account deleted, write to privacy@vincent-ai.app and it will be done.
